Buyer intelligence should end in action.

We are built for complex B2B buying cycles where category, product, pipeline, and competitive questions cross marketing, sales, product, services, security, and leadership.

We transform passive signal-watching into precise commercial decisions.

Buyer intent is useful only when a team can connect it to a real question, a real source, and a next move. ARM brings together approved buyer voice, pipeline evidence, category research, public proof, and validation conditions so teams can see where confidence is forming—or breaking.

The result is not another mention dashboard or unlimited prompt archive. It is a finite map of decision-relevant conversations, the evidence a buyer can inspect, and the work internal owners can complete over the next 90 days.

Category creation and redefinition

02 · LAUNCH

Pipeline acceleration
