Positioning

The generative engines are the new gatekeepers. What they surface about an agent — a founder, a brand, an autonomous system — is now the reputation. When that signal is broken, distorted, or missing, the agent operating behind it inherits the damage.

ARM Agency exists to repair that signal. Not through belief, not through persuasion — through attestation, structure, and record.
