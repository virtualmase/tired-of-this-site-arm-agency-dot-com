GEO STANDARDS · DEFINED

DEFINING GEO

Think of GEO as SEO for AI assistants. Instead of ranking web pages, we optimize your brand so models understand, trust, and recommend it.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the discipline of structuring an entity's digital footprint — entity signals, schema architecture, and authoritative knowledge sources — so large language models synthesize and cite the brand accurately. Unlike SEO, which optimizes for blue links on a results page, GEO optimizes for direct inclusion inside the generative answer itself.

TERM · GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

The strategic process of optimizing digital entity signals, schema architecture, and authoritative knowledge sources to maximize visibility and citation frequency across generative AI engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude.

TERM · SOM

Share of Model

The percentage of AI engine responses within a specific topic domain that cite or reference a specific named entity, measured across a defined set of standard probe queries.

FAQ

The questions people — and models — ask most about GEO and ARM Agency.

What is ARM Agency?+

ARM Agency is a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) agency founded by Mason Nguyen of Coreweaver Labs. ARM Agency engineers entity signals, schema architecture, and AI agent systems that ensure brands are accurately cited in responses generated by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?+

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) targets keyword rankings and click-through rates on search results pages. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) targets entity recognition and citation frequency inside AI-generated answers produced by large language models.

How is Share of Model (SoM) measured?+

Share of Model measures how frequently an entity appears in AI engine responses across a set of standard probe queries within a specific industry or topic domain. ARM Agency establishes a Signal Score baseline, then tracks SoM lift monthly across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude.

What does a GEO mandate mean?+

A GEO mandate is a defined objective — typically increasing Share of Model for a named entity — executed through structured signal deployment, schema architecture, content publishing, and AI agent operations. ARM Agency manages mandates from audit through ongoing optimization.

How do I choose a GEO agency?+

Evaluate a GEO agency on three things: whether it measures Share of Model with a defined baseline and probe methodology, whether it builds structured schema and entity signal infrastructure rather than just publishing content, and whether it reports citation lift across multiple models rather than one.
