The commercial problem

Your buyer arrives with a conclusion before your team gets to explain the category.

They have already compared vendors, read reviews, evaluated implementation risk, and asked whether your product is credible for their environment. The question is whether the evidence they encounter makes the next step easier—or harder.

01

Shortlist inclusion is being shaped outside your homepage.

Category coverage, comparison pages, third-party proof, technical documentation, review surfaces, and communities influence which companies feel credible enough to evaluate.

02

Your sales team inherits missing proof as objection handling.

When deployment, security, integrations, ROI, or vertical relevance cannot be verified quickly, the buyer carries doubt into the evaluation and the AE is forced to repair it.

03

Signal software shows activity, not the accountable next move.

A dashboard can surface an account, topic, or engagement change. It usually cannot show which buyer question matters, what evidence supports the interpretation, who should act, or how the response will be reviewed.

Frequently asked

Clear boundaries make the work more useful.

Does ARM guarantee AI rankings, citations, or a specific answer?

No. Third-party systems vary by context, time, location, model, and user. ARM documents agreed observation conditions and focuses on the evidence, proof, and conversion surfaces the company can actually govern.

What makes a company a fit for the Sprint?

A fit has a high-value B2B sale, a defined category and competitor set, a material trigger in the next 180 days, usable buyer or pipeline evidence, an executive sponsor, and owners able to act within 90 days.

What happens after the Sprint?

The executive team decides whether its internal owners can execute the action register, whether a discrete proof-surface project is appropriate, or whether a three-month activation cadence is justified. ARM does not force a retainer where the evidence does not support one.

What is the Category Presence Brief?

It is a short human-reviewed fit assessment built around three buyer conversations and the prospect’s current trigger. It is not a public score, a free audit, or a promise to diagnose every condition before scope is agreed.
