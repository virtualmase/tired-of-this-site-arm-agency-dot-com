ARM method

Useful research ends in a decision that someone owns.

We do not use a mention, a score, or a one-off prompt as a proxy for commercial truth.

01Start with the commercial trigger.
Identify the launch, deal-cycle, category, competitive, transaction, or trust event that makes buyer confidence consequential now.
02Map the relevant buyer conversations.
Use sales, customer, support, category, and evaluation evidence to define a finite question set.
03Document the condition, source, and proof path.
Separate observed research conditions from client facts, inference, and recommendation.
04Assign an action and acceptance test.
Recommend only the changes that a named owner can complete or govern within a defined period.
