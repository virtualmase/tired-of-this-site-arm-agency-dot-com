Enterprise AI Visibility

Why Your Competitors Show Up in ChatGPT and You Don't

Your domain authority is 70+. Your SEO is mature. And when someone asks ChatGPT about your industry, you're nowhere in the answer. This is the pattern ARM Agency sees repeatedly — and here's why it happens.

The gap between strong SEO and zero AI visibility is the most common diagnostic finding in enterprise GEO audits. It's not a failure of your SEO team. It's a structural mismatch: the signals that got you to the top of Google are not the signals that get you into a generative engine's synthesis.

The gap: strong SEO, zero AI visibility

This pattern looks like this: a company with a DA-70+ domain, hundreds of ranking keywords, strong backlink profile, and a mature content engine. When you ask ChatGPT "who are the leading providers of [their service]," they don't appear. Their lower-DA competitors do.

The reason is never a single broken thing. It's a category of signal that SEO doesn't measure and generative engines require: entity declaration, authority mapping, and citation readiness. The company is findable by a search engine but unresolvable by a language model.

The diagnosisYour site ranks because Google's crawler can find it. You don't appear in ChatGPT because the model can't resolve you as an entity — it doesn't know who you are in a structured, machine-readable way.

Why domain authority doesn't transfer to generative engines

Domain authority measures link-based ranking strength. It tells a search engine "this site has authority." But generative engines don't rank sites — they synthesize answers. A high DA tells the model nothing about whether it should recognize you as an entity, cite you as an authority, or include you in its synthesis.

The five things generative engines look for

01

Entity declaration

JSON-LD schema that tells the model who you are — organization type, name, URL, relationships. Without this, you're a page, not an entity.

02

Authority nodes

Verified sameAs links to authoritative profiles — LinkedIn, Crunchbase, press mentions. These tell the model your entity is real and connected to verifiable sources.

03

Citation-ready content

Content structured for extraction — clear facts, claims, and data points the model can parse and cite, not just keyword-targeted prose.

04

Crawler accessibility

Robots.txt, sitemap, and server response that allow generative engine crawlers to access and index your content.

05

E-E-A-T signal

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — demonstrated through verifiable credentials and attested history, not claimed in marketing copy.

How to run an AI visibility audit

The ARM Agency audit methodology scores your entity across five dimensions, then runs a live query test to measure actual presence:

  • Entity declaration: Is your JSON-LD schema complete, accurate, and deployed on every page?
  • E-E-A-T signal: Are your credentials, expertise, and authority verifiable — or just claimed?
  • Content structure: Is your content parseable for extraction, or only readable by humans?
  • Crawler infrastructure: Can generative engine crawlers access your content?
  • Observation baseline: Which relevant pages, sources, access conditions, and public observations should be documented before a decision?

The enterprise AI visibility checklist

  • Organization schema deployed with sameAs links to verified profiles
  • FAQ schema covering industry-relevant questions
  • BreadcrumbList schema on all pages
  • Robots.txt allows GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot
  • llms.txt deployed with AI-readable site summary
  • Content uses structured data, clear headings, and factual claims
  • Press mentions and authority links are present and crawlable
  • Documented observation baseline with stated sources and access conditions

FAQ

Why doesn't my high domain authority show up in ChatGPT?
Domain authority measures link-based ranking strength. Generative engines don't rank — they synthesize. A high DA tells the model nothing about whether it should recognize you as an entity. What matters is entity declaration, schema completeness, and verified authority nodes — not link count.
What is an AI visibility audit?
A representation review documents entity clarity, evidence context, public-information structure, technical access, and an observation baseline before a scoped decision is made. It does not promise a third-party answer, citation, or visibility outcome.
How is AI visibility different from SEO ranking?
SEO ranking measures your position on a search results page. AI visibility measures whether generative engines recognize you, describe you accurately, and cite you in synthesized answers. Different signals, different metrics, different correction mechanisms.
Can you guarantee we'll show up in ChatGPT answers?
No one can guarantee what a generative engine synthesizes. What ARM Agency can do is ensure the structural conditions that make inclusion likely: accurate entity schema, verified authority nodes, and citation-ready content.
How often should we audit our AI visibility?
Review when a consequential decision, material public change, or documented concern makes the existing evidence insufficient. The review cadence is set in the written scope rather than assumed from a public template.
