Five questions Free · no email required Self-guided orientation
Question 1 of 5
Dimension: Entity record
When a person or system inspects your organization, can it find a clear, supported description of what you do, who is responsible, and which offers are current?
This checks whether the basics — name, description, people, and offers — agree across the pages and records you control.
Question 2 of 5
Dimension: Evidence context
Can independent, credible sources (press, industry publications, repositories) corroborate the expertise and claims you want associated with your brand?
Experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness can be useful review lenses when important claims need credible support.
Question 3 of 5
Dimension: Information clarity
Does your website use structured data (JSON-LD schema markup) to explicitly declare your organization, people, services, and relationships to machines — not just humans?
Accessible visible content and matching markup make the information easier to inspect. A formal review determines which gaps matter to the accepted scope.
Question 4 of 5
Dimension: Technical access
Are your important pages reachable, internally linked, and presented with the technical signals that authorized crawlers and browsers can inspect?
Crawl access requires a direct review of robots.txt, sitemaps, responses, and the pages that carry your important evidence.
Question 5 of 5
Dimension: Observation baseline
Do you have an agreed context for recording the relevant pages, sources, user paths, and observations before deciding what to change?
A stable review context supports documented comparisons over time without promising what any third-party system will do.

The five dimensions, explained

01

Entity record

Can a machine identify you? Names, identifiers, structured data, people, and offers that agree across sources.

02

Evidence context

Do credible external sources corroborate your expertise? Experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness — verified, not asserted.

03

Information clarity

Is your information readable and internally consistent? Visible content, structured data, and accessible paths should agree within the scope being reviewed.

04

Technical access

Can authorized systems reach the relevant pages? Robots.txt, sitemaps, server responses, internal links, and stable HTML are reviewed as technical conditions, not outcomes.

05

Observation baseline

Do you have a documented context for observing the relevant pages, sources, and user paths before changing a material representation condition?
