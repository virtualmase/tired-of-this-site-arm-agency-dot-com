Five questions Free · no email required Instant result
Question 1 of 5
Dimension: Entity Declaration
If someone asks ChatGPT "What is [your company]?" — does it give a correct, specific answer with your actual description, founders, and what you do?
This checks whether generative engines can identify your entity at the most basic level — name, description, people, and offers.
Question 2 of 5
Dimension: E-E-A-T Signal
Can independent, credible sources (press, industry publications, repositories) corroborate the expertise and claims you want associated with your brand?
E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) is what engines use to decide if your claims are believable.
Question 3 of 5
Dimension: GEO Content Structure
Does your website use structured data (JSON-LD schema markup) to explicitly declare your organization, people, services, and relationships to machines — not just humans?
If crawlers can't parse your structure, they guess — and guesses become the narrative engines repeat about you.
Question 4 of 5
Dimension: Crawler Infrastructure
Do AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot, Google-Extended) have explicit permission to access your important pages — and are those pages actually reachable?
Many sites accidentally block the exact crawlers that feed AI engines. A misconfigured robots.txt is the most common invisible break.
Question 5 of 5
Dimension: Share of Model
Are you tracking how often ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity mention your brand across relevant queries — and how that changes over time?
If you're not measuring, you can't tell if signal repair is working. SoM tracking is the metric that replaced rank tracking for the AI era.

The five dimensions, explained

01

Entity Declaration

Can a machine identify you? Names, identifiers, structured data, people, and offers that agree across sources.

02

E-E-A-T Signal

Do credible external sources corroborate your expertise? Experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness — verified, not asserted.

03

GEO Content Structure

Is your content machine-parseable? JSON-LD schema, entity graphs, and citation-ready answers that crawlers can extract without guessing.

04

Crawler Infrastructure

Can AI crawlers actually reach your pages? Robots.txt, sitemaps, server responses, and crawl budget — the plumbing most sites never check.

05

Share of Model

Are you measuring? A stable query set tracks how often engines mention you, cite you, and frame you — across time, against competitors.
