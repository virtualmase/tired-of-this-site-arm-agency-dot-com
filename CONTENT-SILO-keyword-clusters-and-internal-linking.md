## 0. The niche-leverage finding (read this before the cluster tables)

I checked competitive coverage on the two most obvious head terms before building around them:

- **"Generative Engine Optimization" / "GEO agency"** — saturated. Dozens of 2026 "top GEO agencies" roundups already exist (Thrive, Siege Media, WebFX, Victorious, LSEO, and ~10 more), plus Search Engine Journal-caliber publishers own the definitional content. ARM Agency needs a presence here for entity-consistency reasons, but will not win the head term quickly.
- **"Share of Model" (SoM)** — I expected this to be a blue-ocean term ARM could own (it's used as ARM's core metric internally). It isn't anymore. At least seven substantive, well-written explainers published between January and July 2026 (CDP.com, Yotpo, Storylake, Symphonic Digital, Digital Vikingz, LemmiLink, uygen.com) already define and teach it. Worth a supporting page, not a flagship bet.
- **"AI reputation management"** — competitive but winnable. Established players (Status Labs, ReputationX, Blue Ocean Global Tech, Reputation Pros) own the generic term, but their content is uniformly *company/consumer-brand* framed — none of them write for founders specifically, and none frame the discipline as attestation vs. suppression.
- **Autonomous agent / agent-to-agent trust infrastructure** — genuinely open. Zero competing content found for "how do AI agents verify each other's reputation," "Bittensor subnet reputation signal," "agent-to-agent trust protocol," or "mandate chain governance." This is the one cluster where ARM Agency isn't competing against 20 GEO agencies — it's writing the first page that exists. **This is the actual niche leverage**, and it maps directly to Persona 2 and to work you've already shipped (Arctura SN505, Mandate Chain design docs).

Everything below is prioritized around that finding: go first and go deep where there's no competition, and build competent table-stakes coverage everywhere else.

### Link rules applied to every page in the silo
1. Every spoke links **up** to its pillar (anchor: full descriptive title, never "read more")
2. Every spoke links **sideways** to at least 2 sibling spokes in the same pillar
3. Every pillar links **up** to the Resources hub and **up** to the single most relevant homepage section (service card or persona card)
4. Every pillar and spoke carries one **conversion link** to `#pricing` (Signal Audit CTA) — content pages support the funnel, they don't dead-end into themselves
5. The Resources hub links to every live pillar; pillars not yet built are not linked from the hub (no broken links) — see the roadmap in Section 2 instead

## 4. GEO + SEO Content Standard — apply to every page in this silo

Every page built into this silo (this pass and future passes) must ship with:

**SEO fundamentals**
- One primary keyword per page, matched to a single cluster (no cannibalization — see clustering rule)
- Title tag: primary keyword within the first 60 characters
- Meta description: benefit-led, includes primary keyword, ≤155 characters
- One H1 matching page intent; H2s matching supporting/secondary keywords
- Internal links per the rules in Section 3 (up to pillar/hub, sideways to siblings, down to conversion)
- Canonical tag, even on single-silo pages
- Image alt text descriptive, not keyword-stuffed

**GEO layer (per your ARM methodology doc)**
- Entity-led opening: who/what stated declaratively in the first ~50 words, independent of the headline
- Standalone citable paragraphs: every key claim complete without needing surrounding context
- Declarative statements over vague brand language ("ARM Agency defines X as Y," not "we think about X differently")
- `Article` or `TechArticle` JSON-LD schema, author entity included, rendered server-side (never JS-injected)
- FAQ block matched to real query phrasing, with `FAQPage` schema mirroring the visible Q&A exactly
- Every page added to `llms.txt` priority content list and `sitemap.xml` on publish
- Crawler check: confirm GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot are allowed before the page goes live (already true site-wide via `robots.txt`)
